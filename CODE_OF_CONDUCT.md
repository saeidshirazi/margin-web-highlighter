# Community guidelines

Help make Margin useful and welcoming to people with different backgrounds, abilities, and levels of technical experience.

- Be respectful. Discuss the idea or behavior, not the person.
- Give clear, constructive feedback and assume others may be learning.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, spam, or publish someone’s private information.
- Keep discussions relevant, and do not post private research, credentials, or personal browsing data.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, close disruptive discussions, and restrict participation when needed. For a sensitive concern, open an issue asking for a private contact channel without naming people or posting details. Security reports should use the separate [security reporting process](SECURITY.md).
