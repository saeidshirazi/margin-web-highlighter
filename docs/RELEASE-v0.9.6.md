## Saved searches and related highlights

- Use **Save search** to keep the current library view, query, filters, and sort order. Open **Saved searches** to run it again or remove it.
- Choose **Related highlights** on a library quote to find up to five matches based on shared tags and words in quotes and notes. Margin finds matches on your device.

Download your browser package under **Assets**. The unsigned Firefox XPI loads as a temporary add-on. [Install and update guide](https://github.com/saeidshirazi/margin-web-highlighter/blob/main/docs/INSTALL.md)
