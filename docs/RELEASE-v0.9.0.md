## Changes

- Open **Highlights** in the library sidebar to browse individual quotes and notes across your saved sources. Search and combine filters to find a passage, then open its annotation or PDF page.
- Choose **Copy quote with source** to copy a quote and its source title, along with the web address when available and PDF page number when known. This action is also available in the source details pane.

## Install or update

Choose the Chrome, Edge, or Brave ZIP for your browser under **Assets**. The unsigned Firefox XPI installs as a temporary add-on. A source ZIP and SHA-256 checksums are also available.

Back up your library before updating. For an existing unpacked installation, extract into the same folder and click **Reload** on your browser’s extensions page.

[Install and update guide](https://github.com/saeidshirazi/margin-web-highlighter/blob/main/docs/INSTALL.md)
