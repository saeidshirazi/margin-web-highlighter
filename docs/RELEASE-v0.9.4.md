## Proofread notes on websites

Click a highlight on a website to open its annotation popup. Open **Proofread · English** under the note to see up to two suggestions at a time. Apply or ignore them, then use **Save note** to keep your draft. Proofreading runs on your device with the bundled Harper engine.

Download your browser package under **Assets**. The unsigned Firefox XPI loads as a temporary add-on. [Install and update guide](https://github.com/saeidshirazi/margin-web-highlighter/blob/main/docs/INSTALL.md)
