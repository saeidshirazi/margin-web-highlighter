## Pronunciation improvements

Margin remembers the last voice you chose for each language on this browser. The pronunciation toolbar shows **Stop** during playback and switches to **Repeat** when playback ends, with the status alongside the button.

Download your browser package under **Assets**. The unsigned Firefox XPI loads as a temporary add-on. [Install and update guide](https://github.com/saeidshirazi/margin-web-highlighter/blob/main/docs/INSTALL.md)
