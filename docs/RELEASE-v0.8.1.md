Google Drive sync is available in the public downloads. Open **Settings & backups → Continue with Google** and approve access to the private Margin app-data folder.

Chrome users updating from 0.8.0 or earlier should export a full JSON backup first. This build uses a stable extension ID. Keep the old installation until you verify your imported library.

Choose the download for your browser under **Assets**. The Firefox XPI is unsigned and installs temporarily.
