## Proofread notes offline

Open **Proofread note · English** in a library note editor or Quick Note. Suggestions appear when you pause typing. Select an explanation to locate the text, then apply or ignore the suggestion. Proofreading uses the bundled Harper engine, and your draft stays on your device.

Download your browser package under **Assets**. The unsigned Firefox XPI loads as a temporary add-on. [Install and update guide](https://github.com/saeidshirazi/margin-web-highlighter/blob/main/docs/INSTALL.md)
