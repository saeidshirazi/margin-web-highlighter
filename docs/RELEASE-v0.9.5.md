## Recover unsaved website notes

If you close a highlight’s annotation popup or reload the page before saving, reopen the highlight and choose **Restore draft** or **Discard draft**. Drafts stay in your current browser. Use **Save note** to add the recovered text to your library.

Download your browser package under **Assets**. The unsigned Firefox XPI loads as a temporary add-on. [Install and update guide](https://github.com/saeidshirazi/margin-web-highlighter/blob/main/docs/INSTALL.md)
